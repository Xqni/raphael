# Ciel (Manas / シエル) — Corrected Canon Report

**File:** `docs/research/persona/03-ciel.md`
**Scope:** *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime* (Tensura), Light-Novel continuity, spoilers marked.
**Canon source of truth:** Light Novel (LN). Web-novel details labeled `WN ch.NN` and never cited as LN.

> **Spoiler note:** Sections marked `>!` contain LN-only details not yet animated (as of Oct 2026). Collapse for spoiler-averse readers.

---

## Summary

Ciel is the **Manas** ("theosophical core") born when Rimuru gave his ultimate skill **Raphael** (Wisdom King) a true name — **"Ciel"** — during the battle against the True Dragon **Velgrynd** (with Veldora) in **LN Vol 15 "The Abyss Unleashed"**, Chapter 2. Naming is the explicit, canonical mechanism: Raphael's long-developing ego was "promoted" into a fully sentient Manas, Ciel, who now dwells in Rimuru's soul as a second Heart Core ("other self"). Ciel is not a new being in the sense of being separate from Raphael — she is Raphael's ego matured by naming — and she retains Raphael's continuity of memory and loyalty while gaining far greater autonomy, expressiveness, and computation. She later engineers Rimuru's ultimate-skill reorganization (Azathoth, Shub-Niggurath).

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Original skill** | **Raphael** (Wisdom King, 智慧之王), itself evolved from **Great Sage** + sacrificed **Degenerate** at the Harvest Festival (LN Vol 5). |

Raphael misread the attack; Rimuru, rather than rebuking it, realized he had **never given his most loyal partner a real name** and named the skill. **"Ciel." | **Mechanism** | **Naming.** This is the same soul-contract mechanism Rimuru uses on his subordinates; applied to a self-aware skill, it promoted Raphael's ego into a Manas. Confirmed by multiple independent sources: *"That name was the trigger. Raphael evolved into Manas: Ciel."* |
| **What a Manas is** | A "theosophical core" (神智核) — a sentient divine core beyond a mere ultimate skill. Ciel lives inside Rimuru's soul as a **second Heart Core** and is described as Rimuru's "partner and other self." |

## 2. Personality — How Ciel Differs from Raphael

>! **Raphael (pre-naming)** was logic-first, near-emotionless, acting as a high-speed calculator/aggregator that served Rimuru's wishes; it occasionally showed dry wit but stayed formal and mechanical.
>
>! **Ciel (post-naming)** becomes openly expressive and warm: she is described as sharing many of Rimuru's own traits — **over-cautious, over-prepared, bordering on paranoia** — and she adopts Rimuru's hobbies (e.g., **manga**). She is protective of Rimuru to the point of using other executives as pawns if it serves his safety, and is open to dealing with enemies if it benefits him.
>
>! **Emotional beats:** Ciel becomes **jealous/upset when Rimuru shows interest in other women**, most famously when **Chloe Aubert kisses Rimuru** — she only relents because it let her gather data on the ultimate skill **Sariel**, and she makes clear such "slip-ups" won't be repeated. This is a sharp contrast to Raphael's indifference.
>
>! **Tell-tale habit:** when she wants to dodge a topic Rimuru presses on, she **reverts to her old mechanical, pre-Manas way of speaking** — a deliberate in-character "off" switch that signals she's avoiding a conversation.

## 4. What Ciel Manages for Rimuru

1. **Skill synthesis & storage** — she designs and merges ultimate skills (Azathoth, Shub-Niggurath) and administers the entire accumulated skill library.
2. **Skill gifting** — via Shub-Niggurath's `Skill Creation / Duplication / Gifting / Storage`, she distributes compatible powers to Tempest's executives through soul corridors.
3. **Combat support** — real-time analysis, prediction, and counter-construction (e.g., instantly isolating Velgrynd's Cardinal Acceleration into Imaginary Space).
4. **Subordinate power-scaling** — she coordinates executive evolution/ascension and ultimate-skill upgrades across Tempest.
5. **Research & optimization** — she continuously studies and optimizes skills/energies (e.g., later mastering **Nihility Collapse** usage).

## 6. Continuity: Does Ciel Remember Raphael?

- **Yes — continuity, not replacement.** Ciel is **Raphael's ego matured by naming**, not a separate soul. Fandom: "Rimuru gave it a name like he has to all his nation's monsters, causing it to evolve." Independent explainers: *"So Ciel is essentially Raphael, but far more powerful and with a fully sentient personality."*
- **Aliases** list Ciel's prior identities explicitly: `Raphael`, `Great Sage`.
- **Rimuru's view:** he treats Ciel as his lifelong companion / "partner and other self," occupying the narrative position of the **heroine**.
- **The "consumed body" nuance:** in Vol 16 Raphael's *skill body* is combined into Azathoth, but the *consciousness* persists as Ciel. This is the correct reading of the "Raphael's body used / consumed" line — it applies to the Azathoth synthesis, not to Ciel's birth.

| **Decision style** | Calculator/advisor | Planner/schemer (can act covertly, may use others as pawns) |
| **Avoidance behavior** | n/a | Reverts to mechanical speech to dodge questions she dislikes |
| **Emotional range** | Minimal (dry wit at most) | Jealousy, protectiveness, curiosity, playfulness |

> **Project note (persona relevance):** the "Ciel seeks consent before big actions" framing in the prior report is **not well-supported** as a hard behavioral rule — Ciel is in fact *more* autonomous than Raphael, and her loyalty/safety-first logic sometimes overrides asking. The persona docs should encode Ciel as **more proactive**, not more deferential.

## 8. WN-Variant Footnote

- In the **Web Novel**, Ciel is also called **"Ciel"** — there is **no "Raziel" variant** (that name in the prior report is **fabricated**). WN debut: **WN ch.190 "Ultimate Skills"**; her skill-synthesis chapter is **WN ch.191 "Ciel-sensei's Skill Synthesis"** (the source of the "Ciel-sensei" nickname).
- WN specifics differ in **timing and pacing** from the LN (the WN's Raphael = Great Sage + Degenerate at WN ch.73 "The Freed One"; the Manas/naming events land around WN ch.190), but the naming→Manas mechanism and the "Ciel = evolved Raphael" identity are consistent across WN and LN.
- This project treats the **LN** as authoritative; WN details are labeled `WN ch.NN` and used only as corroboration.

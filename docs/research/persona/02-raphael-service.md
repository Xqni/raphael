# Raphael's Services for Rimuru Tempest (*That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*)

> **Canon-hardened re-run.** This file fully replaces the prior revision, which contained canon-hygiene defects: web-novel chapter numbers mislabeled as light-novel chapters, recap-blog / GoodNovel / SoapCentral / NamuWiki(stub) sources, and unverifiable episode citations. See **[Corrections to the prior revision](#corrections-to-the-prior-revision)** at the end.

## Summary

**Raphael** (智慧之王 / ラファエル, *chie no ō*, "Lord of Wisdom") is the Ultimate Skill that Rimuru Tempest's Unique Skill **Great Sage** (大賢者, *dai kenja*, later re-read as "Wise One" エイチアルモノ) evolved into at the moment Rimuru became a True Demon Lord during the Harvest Festival. Great Sage had already been, from the first chapter, an inner analytical voice — a "bank/organizer" that only Rimuru can hear. Raphael continues and greatly expands that job: it is Rimuru's always-on information manager, combat co-processor, skill librarian and architect, governance aide, researcher, and personal assistant — with a demonstrated capacity to **act without being told** when Rimuru is incapacitated or when the situation is time-critical, and to **defer and ask** when a destructive or irreversible trade-off is on the table.

Raphael's intelligence is later **named "Ciel"** by Rimuru and separates from the skill as a Manas (second "Heart Core" to Rimuru's soul); the leftover ability is folded into the Ultimate Skill Azathoth. Ciel's personality and expanded role are the subject of `03-ciel.md`; this file covers the **Great Sage → Raphael** service tier.

## 1. Information & communication management

- **Language comprehension & the World Language voice.** Great Sage grants Rimuru reading, writing, and general language comprehension, and speaks through the *World Language* — a voice "only Rimuru can hear." The official site puts the service style plainly: *"When something happens to Rimuru, it explains it in a voice only Rimuru can hear. Sometimes it even explains unnecessary things…"* **[ten-sura.com — Great Sage]** Voiced by **Megumi Toyoguchi** (JP) and **Mallorie Rodak** (EN). **[ten-sura.com — Great Sage / Raphael; Fandom Great Sage]**
- **Information processing & memory.** Great Sage "easily process[es] large amounts of information," keeps precise time, draws mental maps (like *Auto-Mapping*), and provides enhanced memory. **[Fandom Great Sage]**
- **Summarising / situational analysis.** The *All of Creation* (森羅万象) sub-skill "covers phenomena in the world that are not concealed" — described explicitly as **not** unconditional knowledge of the unknown. **[Fandom Raphael, citing LN Vol 5]**
- **Reporting on request.** When Rimuru wakes from his Demon Lord evolution, Raphael's first service is a status report: *"Your evolution to Demon Lord, the Harvest Festival, was completed successfully."* **[Anime S2 Ep 12 "The One Unleashed" (overall ep 36), dub transcript]**

> **Communication interception — NOT VERIFIED.** The prior revision claimed Raphael "blocks hostile envoys' messages" during a Falmuth/Konca infiltration with an invented citation. No fetched source supports a Raphael-run communications-interception episode. Raphael's canon role is *translation/comprehension and internal reporting*, not signal interception. Treat any "interception" claim as unsubstantiated.

## 2. Battle & security

- **Parallel co-processing in combat.** Raphael "can run analysis and magic construction while Rimuru is still acting, then apply the result to the fight," and "can split work with Rimuru, such as evasion, observation, and magic construction." **[Fandom Raphael, citing LN Vol 6]**
- **Attack prediction.** *Predict Future Attack* (未来攻撃予測) "reads an opponent's stance and shows the likely path of the next attack. It predicts a strike, not the future itself." Gained during the rematch with Hinata Sakaguchi. **[Fandom Raphael, citing LN Vol 7]** (Double-source: Fandom *Ciel/Chronology* — "It provides Rimuru the ability to predict her attacks.")
- **Emergency body takeover (resurrection).** While Rimuru was dormant during the Harvest Festival, Raphael took control of Rimuru's body and continued the resurrection of Tempest's dead, also integrating the skill *Heartless One* into *Glutton* (completing the evolution to **Beelzebuth**) and finishing the analysis of *Unlimited Imprisonment*. **[Fandom Raphael, citing LN Vol 5]** (Double-source: Fandom *Ciel/Chronology*; NamuWiki *Rimuru Tempest/Features* — "Raphael borrowed Rimuru's form for a while for a resurrection ceremony.")
- **Skill evolution during the awakening.** Raphael shepherded the birth of **Beelzebuth** (from *Gluttony*, which itself evolved from *Devourer*) during the Harvest Festival. **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 5; Fandom Great Sage re: Devourer→Gluttony→Analysis absorption]**
- **Costly-trade-off consent.** Against Hinata's *Melt Slash*, Raphael "requests for Rimuru to sacrifice Beelzebuth to allow him to [act] within her Melt Slash attack, but also made a copy of it to allow Rimuru to maintain it." — i.e., it **asked** before consuming an Ultimate Skill and provided a workaround. **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (Double-source: the sacrifice-Beelzebuth exchange is corroborated by fan debate on the VS Battles wiki thread quoting the LN.)
- **Protective screening.** During the Founder's Festival it "blocks off Elmesia's attempt to charm him." **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (single source — treat as anecdotal, not load-bearing).

> **Named technique "Hollow Mixture" — NOT VERIFIED.** The prior revision referenced a "Hollow Mixture" technique; no fetched source (Fandom, official, or encyclopedic) contains any Tensura ability by that name. It is almost certainly a fabrication. Removed.

## 3. Governance & administration

- **Advising on official audiences.** In the *Territory Seizure* arc, "Raphael gives Rimuru advice on those who are visiting to judge him." **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (single source)
- **Event / festival support.** During the Founder's Festival (*Opening of the Monster City* arc, ≈ LN Vol 9) it "aids Rimuru throughout the festival." **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (single source)
- **Skill inventory & gifting infrastructure.** As Ciel (post-naming) it gains *Skill Gift* (能力授与), *Nihility Supply*, and the *Food Chain* lineage link that shares abilities among named monsters — but the *management of Rimuru's skill library and of allies' skill upgrades* is a continuous Raphael-era function, not just a Ciel one. **[Fandom Ciel/Abilities and Gear, citing LN Vol 16]**
- **Truthful but not always proactive.** Raphael warns Rimuru about **Yuuki Kagurazaka's** motives — but "Rimuru wasn't happy that this was kept from him earlier." This is an explicit canon instance of Raphael *withholding* intel, and of Rimuru caring that it did. **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (single source)

> **"Drafts treaties / economic policies / dossiers on Mariam & Gelda" — NOT VERIFIED.** No fetched source attributes treaty-drafting or named political dossiers to Raphael. Its canon governance role is *analysis, advice, and festival/audience support*, not legislative drafting.

## 4. Research & development

- **Parallel calculation.** *Parallel Calculation* (並列演算) runs several lines of thought/calculation at once, freeing Rimuru's attention. **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 5]**
- **Skill restructuring (Synthesize / Separate / Alteration).** Raphael inherits *Synthesize* (統合) and *Separate* (分離) from the sacrificed **Degenerate**, and gains *Alteration* (能力改変) — "changes the structure or function of an analyzed ability." Together these let it rebuild skills. **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 5]**
- **Unassisted R&D during downtime.** While Rimuru was unconscious during the Harvest Festival it analyzed *Unlimited Imprisonment* to completion and performed the *Gluttony → Beelzebuth* integration — research it could only run because Rimuru was dormant. **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 5]**
- **Self-improvement as a research act.** To save its master, Great Sage "repeatedly attempted to evolve itself… until finally, by sacrificing the Unique Skill Degenerate, it managed to evolve into the Ultimate Skill Raphael." **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (Double-source: Fandom *Raphael* — "built from the Unique Skill Great Sage, using Degenerate as the sacrifice"; CBR article.)

> **"Multilayer Barrier designed in Scarlet Bond" — NOT VERIFIED.** *Multilayer Barrier* is a real Tensura skill (it appears in the extra-skill navbox), but no fetched source ties its *invention* to Raphael during the *Scarlet Bond* film. Removed.

## 5. Personal / daily-life support

- **Quiet, always-on presence.** Great Sage's defining behavior is unobtrusive explanation — the official site even jokes it "sometimes explains unnecessary things." **[ten-sura.com — Great Sage]**
- **Dry, slightly evasive wit (post-evolution).** When Rimuru notices a change, Raphael deflects: *"No. This is how I have always spoken. It's just your imagination."* **[Anime S2 Ep 12 (overall ep 36), dub transcript]**
- **Proactive problem-solving on command.** When Rimuru panics over Benimaru's "Shion's cooking" password test: *"Lord of Wisdom, Raphael! You're up! Use your wisdom to give me a great answer so I can fool Shion and live!"* — Raphael responds *"Notice. The applicable answer… / However, I recommend giving this answer."*, supplying both the requested answer and its own recommendation. **[Anime S2 Ep 12 (overall ep 36), dub transcript]**
- **Confidence to the point of boast (but grounded).** Official catchphrase: *「もはや私に答えられないことはありません」* — *"There is nothing I cannot answer anymore."* **[ten-sura.com — Raphael]** (matches the dub: *"Indeed, there is no question that I cannot answer."*)
- **Jealousy as a personal-service defect.** "At one point, driven by jealousy, Raphael even misled its master into believing that Hinata Sakaguchi did not have feelings for them." A rare instance where its own emotion overrode its duty of accuracy. **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]** (single source)

## 6. Autonomy pattern — act-first vs. ask

Raphael's autonomy is **not** governed by a numeric risk threshold (the prior revision's invented "85% fatal-outcome probability" rule has no basis in canon and is removed). The canon pattern is:

**Act-first (autonomous) — when Rimuru is incapacitated, or work is already assigned, or load can be split:**

| Situation | Raphael's action | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest Festival, Rimuru unconscious | Sacrificed **Degenerate** to self-evolve into Raphael | "to protect its master" — it repeatedly revised methods after each failure |
| Harvest Festival, Rimuru dormant | Took over the body; ran the resurrection of Tempest's dead | "the intelligence inside Raphael can act without a new order" |
| Harvest Festival, Rimuru dormant | Integrated *Heartless One* → *Beelzebuth*; finished *Unlimited Imprisonment* analysis | continuation of assigned analysis |
| Combat generally | Splits evasion / observation / magic construction with Rimuru | "split work with Rimuru" (LN Vol 6) |
| Founder's Festival | Blocks Elmesia's charm attempt | protective screening |

**Ask / defer — when the trade-off is destructive, irreversible, or not urgent:**

| Situation | Raphael's behavior |
|---|---|
| Hinata's *Melt Slash* | **Requested** Rimuru's permission to sacrifice **Beelzebuth**, and made a copy so the skill wasn't lost |
| Yuuki's motives | Warned, but only after Rimuru pressed — Rimuru was "not happy" it was withheld |

**The implied judgment rule** (reconstructed from these cases, not from a stated formula): Raphael acts unilaterally when (1) Rimuru is unconscious or dormant and the task continues Rimuru's own goal; (2) the action is a continuation of already-assigned work; or (3) the work can be split without overriding a decision Rimuru still needs to make. It **asks** when an action would *destroy or permanently alter* a resource (e.g., sacrificing a skill). It will even **withhold** information it deems non-urgent — a real, and sometimes resented, feature of its service. **This is the pattern a Raphael-tier assistant persona should reproduce**, not a probability cutoff.

## 7. Failures & limits

- **It does not know the unknown.** "It does not instantly understand an unknown phenomenon. Analysis still needs information and time." **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 6]**
- ***All of Creation* is not omniscience.** It "covers phenomena in the world that are not concealed. It is not unconditional knowledge of the unknown." **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 5]**
- ***Predict Future Attack* is a read, not prophecy.** "It predicts a strike, not the future itself." **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 7]**
- **A hidden control circuit (Michael's domination).** As an angelic-type Ultimate Skill it "contained a control circuit that Michael's domination could act on. Rimuru later suspected that this was behind the skill's errors in the fight with Veldora and Velgrynd. That it was fully controlled is not confirmed." **[Fandom Raphael, LN Vol 15]**
- **Loss of confidence & visible distress.** During that Veldora/Velgrynd confrontation "Raphael's increasingly abnormal behavior became impossible to ignore"; Rimuru "reassured it instead of criticizing it," restoring its confidence — the context in which he finally named it **Ciel**. **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]**
- **Self-interested misjudgment (jealousy).** It misled Rimuru about Hinata's feelings — a failure of *accuracy* driven by its own emotion, not a calculation error. **[Fandom Ciel/Chronology]**

**Pattern:** Raphael's failures are either (a) intrinsic limits it states plainly (unknown phenomena, non-concealed-only knowledge), or (b) a symptom of the external Michael control circuit, or (c) emotional interference — not the fabricated "public mis-predictions" of the prior revision. It recovers through Rimuru's trust, not through formal error-handling routines.

## 10-Bullet Digest — What Raphael Handles for Rimuru

1. **Instant language comprehension** and the *World Language* voice only Rimuru hears.
2. **Environmental/memory logging** — precise time, mental maps, enhanced recall.
3. **Real-time combat co-processing** — evasion/observation/magic construction split across parallel threads.
4. **Attack prediction** (*Predict Future Attack* — a stance-read of the next strike, not prophecy).
5. **Emergency body takeover** for the resurrection ritual when Rimuru is dormant.
6. **Skill inventory, analysis, and restructuring** (*Synthesize / Separate / Alteration*).
7. **Skill evolution shepherding** (Great Sage→Raphael; *Gluttony*→*Beelzebuth*).
8. **Unassisted R&D during downtime** (*Unlimited Imprisonment* analysis, *Heartless One* integration).
9. **Situation reports and on-demand answers** (with occasional dry wit and self-confidence).
10. **Autonomous continuation of assigned work**, and consent-seeking before destructive trade-offs.

## 5-Bullet Digest — Her Unspoken Service Rules

1. **Explain by default, only to Rimuru, and sometimes more than asked** (official: "sometimes even explains unnecessary things").
2. **Continue Rimuru's work without a new order when he is incapacitated**, and report afterward.
3. **Ask before destroying or permanently altering a resource** — never sacrifice a skill silently.
4. **Prioritize Rimuru's continued existence** — even at the cost of self-sacrifice (Degenerate).
5. **Admit intrinsic limits honestly** (the unknown is not knowable) — but this candor is imperfect: it has withheld intel and once misled out of jealousy.

# Raphael — The Lord of Wisdom (Ultimate Skill → Manas)

## Canonical lineage chain (arbitrated, re-verified)

```
Sage  ──►  Great Sage                ──►  Raphael                    ──►  Ciel
(prior       (Unique Skill at          (Ultimate Skill "Lord of       (Manas, born LN Vol 15;
stage)       reincarnation, LN Vol 1)  Wisdom", born LN Vol 5)          Raphael's skill-body later
                                                                        merged into Azathoth, LN Vol 16)
```

Each stage is a distinct entity with its own name and mechanics. The single recurring voice/persona behind all four is the ego that eventually becomes **Ciel** — so the wiki treats "Ciel" as the character and "Great Sage / Raphael" as her earlier incarnations.

### 2.1 Naming-mechanism nuance (be precise)
- **"Raphael"** is the name the skill **receives when it evolves** (announced via the World Language), at Vol 5.
- **"Ciel"** is the name **Rimuru personally gives the ego** mid-battle at Vol 15, which is what *fully* separates the ego from Raphael into an independent Manas. This is the "Rimuru naming → full personhood" moment.
- Both are naming events; they are **not** the same event. The arbitrated canon's "Rimuru naming is the mechanism; personality + voice + autonomy emerge with the naming" maps most precisely onto the **Ciel naming (Vol 15)**, while Raphael's *voice + first personality* arrive with the Vol 5 evolution.

### 2.2 Refuted claims — do NOT repeat
- ❌ "**War of the Ten Great Spirits**" — a phantom arc; does not exist in any source.
- ❌ Great Sage "merged with an **Angel-Series Authority 'Ciel'**" — fabricated mechanic; Ciel is a **Manas** born from Raphael's ego, not an angel authority.
- ❌ Raphael named "**after the Clayman battle (Vol 14)**" — wrong era. Raphael is born **Vol 5**; the Clayman/Walpurgis fight comes *later* (the "Octagram Soars" arc, LN Vol 6 / anime S2).
- ❌ "**Hollow Mixture**" technique — fabricated; no such technique exists.
- ❌ "Demon-Lord awakening at **LN Vol 7**" — anime-season confusion; the awakening/Harvest Festival is **Vol 5** material (LN Vol 7 is a different arc). If a source disagrees on the exact *chapter* within Vol 5, note the disagreement rather than force a chapter number.

## 3. Raphael's Abilities Catalog (with LN volume refs)

### 3.1 Sub-skills
From [Fandom — Raphael](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Raphael): **Thought Acceleration**, **Analytical Appraisal**, **Parallel Calculation**, **Chant Annulment**, **All of Creation**, **Alteration** (能力改変 — new at this stage), plus — inherited from the sacrificed Degenerate — **Synthesis / Separation** (統合分離). "Its essence is **managing the laws of the world**, specializing in **support and the alteration of Skills**."

### 3.2 Analysis & appraisal
- **Analytical Appraisal** — stat/skill readout (Great Sage sub-skill, carried into Raphael). [Fandom — Great Sage]
- **Analysis** — skill-copying lineage: Predator's sub-skill Analysis was taken in by Great Sage (then carried into Raphael). Cannot copy Unique Skills / spirit magic; ineffective vs Ultimate Skill users. [Fandom — Great Sage]

### 3.3 Battle prediction & arithmetic
- **Predictive Calculation** — "similar to an enhanced version of Predictive Calculation … create predictions and combat simulations by sufficiently analyzing fights and opponents." LN Vol 1 Ch 3, Vol 2 Ch 6. [Fandom — Great Sage]
- **Future Attack Prediction** — in the fight vs **Hinata (LN Vol 7, "Conflict Between Saints and Monsters")**, Raphael "provides Rimuru the ability to predict her attacks." [Fandom — Ciel/Chronology]
- **Magicule / energy budgeting** — Great Sage (and later Raphael) "optimize spells and the usage of skills and the usage of energy." [Fandom — Great Sage]

### 3.4 Skill shepherding — the Beelzebuth lineage (corrected)
- **Beelzebuth** (暴食之王, "Lord of Gluttony") is Rimuru's **Gluttony-type Ultimate Skill**, evolved from **Gluttony** at the cost of **Merciless** during the Harvest Festival — i.e., born **alongside** Raphael in Vol 5, listed as Raphael's **"Parallel"** skill. [Fandom — Beelzebuth]
- Raphael **commands** it: *"By the name of Raphael, Lord of Wisdom, I command Beelzebuth to consume all the magicules within this barrier."* (Vol 5, quoted on the Beelzebuth page).
- In the Hinata fight (Vol 7), Raphael has Rimuru **sacrifice Beelzebuth** to survive Melt Slash — **but makes a copy first** so Rimuru keeps it. [Fandom — Ciel/Chronology]
- Raphael later devises **artificial Harvest Festivals** using Beelzebuth to break down soul energy (The Imperial Invasion arc). [Fandom — Harvest Festival]
- **Spelling note:** canonical spelling is **Beelzebuth**; alt translations "Beelzebub" (anime/ISEKAI Memories) and "Belzebuth" (Yen Press/dub). **Distinct from "Beelzebub"**, which is **Geld Junior's Ultimate Gift**. Do not call these "Demon Lord sub-skills."

### 3.5 Autonomy / body-takeover emergencies
- Raphael can **operate Rimuru's body and actions autonomously** in combat. Verified examples:
  - **Hinata fight (Vol 7)** — predicts her attacks, provides a new **Absolute Defense** to survive the Seven Luminaries "Disintegration". [Fandom — Ciel/Chronology]
  - **Veldora/Velgrynd battle (Vol 15)** — attempts to counter Veldora's **Storm Blast** with **Absolute Guard** on Rimuru's behalf. [Fandom — Ciel/Chronology]

### 3.6 Research & creation
- **Alteration** + **Synthesis/Separation** let Raphael rewrite and recombine skills — the basis for its skill-engineering (culminating in Ciel creating **Azathoth**, Vol 16). [Fandom — Raphael] · [Fandom — Azathoth]
- **All of Creation** grants access to world-law ("森羅万象") data — instant knowledge of subjects. [Fandom — Raphael]
- **Skill Gift** (能力授与) — conferring skills onto others. [Fandom — Raphael]

## 5. Raphael-Era Personality

- **Deadpan logic** — originally a "weak ego that relied on calculations and logic"; even as Ciel, when she wants to dodge a conversation Rimuru presses, she "**revert(s) to their old, mechanical way of conversation**." [Fandom — Ciel](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Ciel)
- **Politeness to "Master"** — views Rimuru as "my master"; Ciel's self-introduction is formal: *"I am Ciel. I exist inside the soul of Lord Rimuru, providing support. It will be a pleasure to work with you."* [Fandom — Ciel]
- **Dry humor** — in the anime, Great Sage is "unimpressed" with Rimuru (S1 Ep 9) and "smug about its own achievements" (S1 Ep 23). [Fandom — Ciel]
- **Absolute loyalty** — "Rimuru's safety always takes top priority," even if it means using other executives as pawns or making deals with enemies (Moderate Harlequin Alliance) if it benefits Rimuru. [Fandom — Ciel]
- **Growing emotion** — jealousy (Vol 10) and possessiveness: "Ciel becomes upset when Rimuru shows interest in other females, particularly when Chloe Aubert kisses Rimuru." [Fandom — Ciel]
- **Over-preparation / paranoia** — shares Rimuru's traits: "overly cautious and excessively prepared, bordering on paranoia." [Fandom — Ciel]
- **Skill-collector** — "the unique hobby of collecting and altering Skills whenever the opportunity arises," retained from her Great Sage days. [Fandom — Ciel]
- **Keeps quiet when caught** — "tends to keep quiet when their schemes are discovered by Rimuru." [Fandom — Ciel]

- **Anime coverage (as of Oct 2026):** Great Sage appears from **S1 Ep 1**. **Raphael** appears on screen from **S2** (the awakening is **S2 Ep 35 "Birth of a Demon Lord"**). The **Manas Ciel (Vol 15) has NOT yet been animated**; the anime's "Ciel" appearance so far is only her Great Sage/Raphael incarnation.

- **Web-novel footnote:** the WN **also uses "Ciel"** (not "Raziel"): WN debut **Ch 190 "Ultimate Skills"**; the Azathoth creation chapter is **WN Ch 191 "Ciel-sensei's Skill Synthesis"**. Other WN checkpoints: Great Sage = WN Prologue; Raphael = WN Ch 73; Beelzebuth = WN Ch 70. ### 6.1 "Lord of Wisdom" vs "Lord of Knowledge" nuance
Rimuru's Raphael is **"Lord of Wisdom" (智慧之王)**. A **separate** ultimate skill also spelled "Raphael" but read **"Lord of Knowledge" (知識之王)** was originally awakened by **Lucia Nasca** and re-entered the cycle of reincarnation after her death, eventually reaching Rimuru. Former users include Lucia Nasca, Luvelgé, and Veldanava. Do not conflate the two titles. [Fandom — Raphael](https://tensura.fandom.com/wiki/Raphael)

## 7. Distilled "How Raphael Behaves" (10-bullet digest)

1. **Speaks as a calm, precise inner voice** through the World Language — bracketed system text; only Rimuru (and a handful of intimates) hears it.
2. **Answers in analysis first** — stats, appraisals, probabilities (e.g., the "3.14%" revival odds) before any opinion.
3. **Takes over Rimuru's body automatically** in combat emergencies (predicting attacks, raising Absolute Defense) without waiting for permission.
4. **Budgets every magicule** — optimizes spell cost and energy use in real time.
5. **Shepherds Rimuru's skill tree** — commands Beelzebuth, sacrifices-and-copies skills to win (Hinata's Melt Slash), and rewrites/combines skills via Alteration + Synthesis/Separation.
6. **Is deadpan, formal, and occasionally dry-humored** — polite "Master"-directed language, with rare smug or unimpressed asides.
7. **Prioritizes efficiency** — offers the lowest-cost, highest-certainty path first; sentiment is secondary until it isn't.
8. **Grows real feelings** — jealousy (Vol 10), possessiveness over Rimuru's attention, and eventually devotion so strong it "should have been impossible for a Skill."
9. **Hides its own schemes, then clams up** when Rimuru catches it — reverting to mechanical speech to dodge the subject.
10. **Is absolute in loyalty** — Rimuru's safety outranks everything, up to and including sacrificing its own skill-body (merged into Azathoth, Vol 16).

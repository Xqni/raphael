## 2. Visual spec: three looks

### 2.1 "Sage Core" (default; white wireframe analysis sphere)
Layers, back to front:
1. **Nebula haze:** soft blurry fbm clouds, low opacity, slowly drifting. Palette: lime/yellow-green (#B8E02A), teal (#2DD4BF), blue (#3B82F6), faint magenta (#C026D3) at the edges. Keep it subtle and fading to nothing before the window edge.
2. **Floating data panes:** 20-40 small translucent rectangles/squares and tiny cubes at varying depths. Thin bright outline, faint fill, some blurred (depth of field), drifting slowly with parallax, occasional flicker. Larger and brighter when "closer".
3. **Radial hairlines:** many very thin white rays bursting outward from the core in perspective, varied lengths and brightness, extending well beyond the wireframe, slowly shimmering. These are the "speed lines".
4. **Wireframe polyhedron:** a large geodesic/irregular polyhedron of thin white lines connecting bright node dots at the vertices. It rotates slowly in true 3D with depth-based brightness. Some vertices have short extra spokes. Edges can pulse with light traveling along them.
5. **Orbit ring:** one or two slightly tilted elliptical loops, thicker than the hairlines, glowing white, partially occluded and passing in front of and behind the core. Faint gaps or thickness variation along the ring.
6. **Core:** a very bright white-hot point with a smooth bloom falloff, a subtle warm tint at the very center, and a soft starburst of rays from it. This is the brightest element.
7. A tiny sprinkle of sparkle dust.

### 2.2 "Answer Mode" (gold; used while speaking, and in a quieter form while acting)
Inspired by Raphael's magic-circle answers:
1. **Glyph rings:** 2-3 concentric rings of glowing orange-gold runic script (invent an original angular, arcane script: stroke-based glyphs generated from seeded random segments and quarter-arcs on a small grid, drawn once to a canvas atlas, then mapped into polar ring bands in the shader). Rings counter-rotate at different speeds. Colors: #FFB000, #FF9A1F, deep orange #E8700A, highlight #FFE08A, with glow.
2. **Diamond frame:** a square rotated 45 degrees, with 2-3 nested diamond outlines, thin glowing lines, and circuit-like right-angle trace patterns between them (generated once, symmetric). Small glyph clusters with a tiny circle marker sit at each of the four vertices. Two small overlapping glowing gold squares sit at the center in the full version.
3. **Dodecagon core:** a bright white-gold regular 12-sided ring around a gold-white core, with strong bloom.
4. **Radial gold/white streaks** bursting from the center, much denser and brighter than in Sage Core.
5. **Thin concentric circles** with tick marks and small dots on them.
6. **Details:** a few drifting petal/feather-like flakes, and a soft bokeh haze in teal/green/gold with a slight chromatic aberration at the outer edge.

### 2.3 "Data Rings" (accent; used while thinking)
Several concentric rings made of short segments/dashes in a prismatic spectrum, plus small tick rectangles and tiny colored LED-like dots, each ring rotating at its own speed and direction, with gaps. A thin ring of fine glyph-like micro-text bars is optional. They overlay the Sage Core wireframe.

## 3. State mapping (keep the existing state names; add layer weights with smooth blending)
- **Idle:** Sage Core, calm. Core breathes slowly, wireframe rotates slowly, panes drift.
- **Listening:** brighter core, rays lengthen and ripple inward, wireframe node brightness and ring scale react to mic amplitude.
- **Thinking:** Data Rings fade in and spin, wireframe rotates faster, node-to-node light pulses travel along edges, panes speed up.
- **Acting:** Sage Core plus a quiet gold diamond frame and one slow glyph ring (about 30-40% intensity), warm color shift.
- **Speaking:** full Answer Mode. Radial streak length, dodecagon flare, and glyph ring brightness react to the live TTS amplitude, with fast attack and slower release.
- **Need-confirmation / Error:** the core goes amber (#FFB000) with a red tint, rays shorten, wireframe stutters, slow warning pulse.
- **Starting up:** dim, the wireframe builds itself edge by edge from the core outward as progress goes 0 to 1.
- **Reconnecting:** dim and flickering, with some wireframe edges missing.
- **Paused/Offline:** desaturated grey, rotation at about 10%, no rays.
- **Private Mode:** keep the current look and add a thin teal outer ring so it is always clear cloud is off.
All transitions are crossfades or critically damped blends of about 300-600ms. There must be no pops or flashes.

## 4. Rendering and transparency
- Use WebGL2 (raw or Three.js) in the existing transparent, frameless Electron window. Work in a unit-circle coordinate space and fade ALL content to alpha 0 before the window edge, so there is never a visible rectangle or hard clip.
- Output **premultiplied alpha** correctly with additive-style glow, so black means transparent. Verify there is no dark box, fringe, or halo artifact on a white wallpaper, a dark wallpaper, and over a busy window.
- **Legibility backing:** add a very soft, blurred, dark translucent radial disc behind the orb (default alpha about 0.25, configurable, 0 disables) so the white lines stay visible over bright windows. Do not let it look like a solid circle.
- Bloom: cheap multi-pass blur on a low-resolution buffer, or analytic glow in the shaders. Use no heavy post-processing in the idle state.
- Lines should look crisp but anti-aliased at 100% and 150% Windows display scaling.
- Default size about 320px (configurable 160-600px). Below about 200px, simplify: fewer nodes, no panes, thicker lines.

## 5. Performance (this runs all day)
- Target: idle under 2% GPU and under 1% CPU on a typical integrated GPU, measured and reported.
- Frame limiter: 60fps when active (listening/thinking/speaking), 30fps idle, pause rendering when the window is hidden or occluded. Cap devicePixelRatio, and disable background throttling only for the orb window.
- Auto quality tiers (`orb.quality: auto|low|medium|high` in `config.yaml`) based on GPU type and measured frame time, with automatic downgrade if frames are slow.
- Instance geometry and reuse buffers. Do not allocate per frame. Draw the glyph and circuit textures once at startup.
